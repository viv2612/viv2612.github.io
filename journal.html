Fall 2025

freshman year growth reflection

My first semester in college! This semester definitely rocked my world at first, but over time I learned to acclimatize. Finding a routine and learning how to say no to things was something I figured out the hard way.

As usual, I struggled with comparing myself to others and feeling proud of my achievements. Starting as early as elementary school, so many things were framed as competitions with clear winners: science fair, Battle of the Books, and so on. I learned to measure my own capabilities and success by how much I outperformed or underperformed someone else.

Although I'm still a competitive person (as you can definitely tell from my CliftonStrengths), I am learning to redefine success for myself! A quote I discovered that really hit me was: “own lane, own race, own pace.” There will always be someone smarter, stronger, and more charismatic than me, but each of us are on our own unique journey.

Unrelated to all of that, I would highly recommend that every college freshman take a light course load their first fall semester! Spend time getting involved on campus and expanding your network. I think we, myself included, sometimes forget how much of a privilege it is to experience moments that we will later look back on so fondly and wish we could relive. Yes, you’re going to college for a degree, but you’re also going for the community and connections.

Do not get too tied down to the version of yourself on paper; your resume will build with time!!
