Biography ～～～～～～～～～～～～～～～～

Hi! My name is Vivienne, and I am a second-year marketing major at the University of Florida. I freelance college application reviews and serve as a social media manager for three organizations on campus. In my free time, you can find me behind a camera, editing on Canva, hanging out with friends, thrifting at local shops, and trying out new foods!

Currently, I'm building my skills in Canva and Lightroom. My next goal is to complete Adobe certifications, where I will learn how to use Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, and Firefly. I love reviewing analytics and creating new designs to keep content fresh and trendy. I am a huge advocate for focusing on brand voice and style.

I look forward to continuing to build my career in marketing and enhancing my skill set! I am passionate about using my strengths, leadership, and creativity to make a meaningful contribution to the world around me :)
